The Business

What Zaggle actually sells, and why the unit economics work the way they do.

Zaggle runs a spend-management platform — prepaid cards, expense automation, and rewards infrastructure sold to mid-size and large Indian enterprises on a SaaS-plus-transaction-fee model.

Roughly 60% of revenue is subscription/platform fee, recurring and sticky once a company embeds Zaggle into its expense workflow; the remainder is transaction-linked, which is what has driven the recent growth acceleration as card spend volumes scale with each cohort of enterprise clients.

"The switching cost isn't the software — it's the finance team's habit. Once expense approval routes through our platform, ripping it out is a project, not a decision." — Management commentary, Q4 FY26 earnings call

The competitive set spans point solutions (expense-only tools) and larger horizontal SaaS platforms bundling spend management as a feature — Zaggle's bet is that a purpose-built platform with card issuance built in beats either.
