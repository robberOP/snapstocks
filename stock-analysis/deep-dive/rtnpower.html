01

Business & Moats

A core baseload energy supplier to Maharashtra with high-efficiency coal plants.

RattanIndia Power Limited (RTNPOWER) operates coal-based thermal power plants with an installed capacity of 2,700 MW (Phase 1: 1,350 MW commissioned at Amravati, Maharashtra). It sells 1,200 MW via a Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) to MSEDCL, and additional capacity in the open market.

Key Business Moats

Stable Revenue

1,200 MW PPA with MSEDCL ensures consistent offtake and regulated returns over the long term.

Captive Infrastructure

Fuel Supply Agreement (FSA) with SECL for 6.10 MMT of coal, 60 MCM water allocation, and a dedicated 35 km railway siding.

04

Margins & Expenses

Significant deleveraging drops finance costs, enabling PAT positivity.

The turnaround is most visible in the bottom line, driven by massive deleveraging which slashed finance costs, and steady fuel costs.

Finance Costs Dropping

Interest expense has significantly decreased from ₹148.17 Cr in Q1 FY26 to ₹85.93 Cr in Q1 FY27. This is the direct result of debt restructuring.

PAT Turnaround

The company turned PAT positive, posting ₹44.36 Cr in Q1 FY27 compared to a loss of ₹14.60 Cr in the same quarter last year.

Balance Sheet & Cash Flow

The balance sheet has undergone a major transformation. Total debt was reduced from a staggering ₹21,020 Cr in FY23 down to ₹3,710 Cr in FY26. Net debt currently stands at ₹3,627 Cr.

06

Management

Strong execution on operational turnaround.

Promoter holding remains healthy at 50.16%. The management successfully restructured massive debt over the last few years and brought the plant to top-tier operational metrics, achieving an impressive 92% PLF and ensuring steady cash flows.

07

Valuation & Outlook

Bull and Bear scenarios.

At a CMP of ₹7.89, RattanIndia Power trades at a P/E of 37.57 and a Price-to-Book of 0.91, reflecting a market that is slowly pricing in its turnaround story.

Bull Case

Resolution of regulatory receivables (MSEDCL Change in Law), sustained 90%+ PLF, and continued interest cost reduction could lead to multi-fold PAT expansion, pushing the stock price closer to its book value of ₹8.66 or higher.

Bear Case

Adverse ruling in Supreme Court against the APTEL order, coal supply disruptions, or inability to service the remaining ₹3,700 Cr debt could stress equity value and halt the turnaround momentum.
